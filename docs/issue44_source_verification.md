## Why `VN003_HANOI_OPENAQ_7441` Is Approved as an Independent Station

1. **No False Station Coalescing:** Earlier review blocked replacing `VN002` with `7441` because `7441` does not document the internal hardware maintenance log of the US Embassy BAM-1020 monitor. Designing `7441` as its own station identity (`VN003_HANOI_OPENAQ_7441`) resolves that blocker cleanly: station-specific analysis of `VN003` does not assume or claim that `VN003`, `VN002`, and `VN001` are a single continuous physical instrument.
2. **Full Reproducibility Without Credentials:** `python scripts/build_extended_historical_dataset.py` downloads all 903 daily S3 files directly from `openaq-data-archive.s3.amazonaws.com` and the matching 2022–2025 ERA5 meteorology from `archive-api.open-meteo.com`, verifying SHA-256 hashes and canonical schemas automatically.
3. **Transparent Coverage Reporting:** All missing months in S3 (e.g., Jul–Aug 2022, Mar 2023) and sentinel blocks (e.g., Apr 2022 `-999.0` values) are preserved as `NaN` on the station's hourly grid (`28,678` hours; `18,208` observed valid hours = `63.49%`; `10,470` missing hours = `36.51%`) and reported per month and per year in `reports/issue44_openaq_7441_manifest.json`.
